# Phishing Email Example

## Example Message

**Subject:** Urgent: Your Account Requires Verification

**From:** security@example-support.com

Hello,

We detected unusual activity on your account.

To prevent your account from being suspended, please verify your account immediately using the link below:

**[Verify Your Account]**

If you do not complete the verification, access to your account may be restricted.

Thank you,  
Account Security Team

---

## Warning Signs

This message contains several phishing indicators:

1. **Urgent language** – It creates pressure to act immediately.
2. **Unexpected security alert** – The recipient may not have requested any account verification.
3. **Suspicious sender** – The sender address does not clearly belong to a trusted organization.
4. **Credential request** – The message attempts to make the user visit a verification page.
5. **Threat of account suspension** – Fear is used to encourage quick action.

## Safe Response

Do not click the link or provide credentials.

Instead:

- Verify the sender independently.
- Visit the organization's official website directly.
- Report the message as phishing.
- Delete the message after reporting it.

## Security Lesson

**Never let urgency replace verification.**

When a message asks for credentials, money, or sensitive information, stop and verify the request through a trusted channel.
